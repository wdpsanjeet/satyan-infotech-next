Development team to boost Your Business

Why should you hire dedicated?

Developing a website or mobile app for your business is by no means a simple project. It requires a team of professionals to ensure that the website or app is created to look great and work in a smooth manner. As either presents itself as the face of your brand or business, it is important that you get them right by choosing the ideal kind of experts to work on them. There are two ways you can go about this: either create an in-house development team or hire dedicated resources as an external team. An in-house development team is just that - a group of web and mobile developers that are full-time employees of your company. A dedicated resource (or resources, as the case may be), are a team of professional developers that work as full-time employees of a developing agency, who will work on your project. Think of it as hiring a freelancer, but instead of just one individual, it’s a group of people with different expertise who will take over the development of your app, website or software. The ‘dedicated’ part comes in because generally during the course of your project, those developers and experts will not work on any other client’s app or website. All of their focus, efforts and time will be dedicated to working solely on your project. As such, these resources will always be available to you for any of the requirements your project may demand.

Save Time and Cost

When you hire dedicated mobile/web developers as your external resource, they will be able to divide the project into smaller tasks and have multiple teams working on the same app or website. Work can also be done simultaneously on both your app and website, with specialized experts working on the front end and back end at the same time. This is how deployment times are sped up when you hire dedicated web developers or dedicated mobile app developers. The project manager will keep you informed with regular updates and you can also expect iterations in a timely manner. Research suggests that when you hire dedicated web developers or dedicated mobile app developers, you can save as much as 40% on your project costs. This is because an in-house development team means that they are your full-time employees with regular salaries and perks. A dedicated web developer, on the other hand, will work pretty much as a freelancer, in that, you have to only pay for their services. As we said above, development requires people with different types of expertise. To hire experts in different fields as full-time employees, you should have the kind of work volume that will justify such an investment. It also takes much more effort to recruit such a team.
